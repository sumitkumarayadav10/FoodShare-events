ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why FoodShare Events? & How It Works

Traditional food donations rely on unorganized phone calls, leading to delays and food spoilage. Our centralized web platform automates food rescue with live expiry tracking and GPS navigation.

🏨
STEP 1
Donor Lists Surplus

Event hosts, banquet halls, or restaurants post food details, meal count, pickup address, and safe consumption hours.

🤝
STEP 2
NGO Claims Meals

Nearby verified NGOs and shelters view live alerts and claim the meals needed for their community beneficiaries.

🛵
STEP 3
Volunteer Pickup

Volunteer riders accept the delivery task, open one-click Google Maps navigation, and collect the food safely.

🏆
STEP 4
Delivery & Certificate

Food reaches needy people before expiry, and Donors & Volunteers earn verified Social Impact Certificates.

⏳
Live Food Freshness Timer

Real-time countdown ensures perishable event food is picked up and distributed while 100% safe to eat.

📍
Google Maps Route Integration

Instant turn-by-turn directions help volunteer delivery riders locate venues and NGOs without delay.

🌍
CO₂ & Sustainability Metrics

Tracks every meal rescued and calculates greenhouse gas emissions saved from landfills.

🥘 Live Surplus Food Listings

Real-time feed of active donations, assigned riders, and completed deliveries
